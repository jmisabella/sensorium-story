## Notes on Chapter Breaks

- Chapters 1–7 (Act One) should feel like a world working under strain — the horror is latent
- Chapters 8–16 (Act Two) should feel like something unraveling that has been unraveling all along — each beat a notch further
- Chapters 17–20 (Act Three) should be short. No release. The suspended choice is not a failure to end; it IS the ending.
- The Epilogue is the exhale, but the lungs don't fully clear.
- Actual chapter lengths will vary significantly. The Freeze chapter may be one of the longest in the book; some Act Three chapters may be very short.
- These chapter groupings are working logistics, not constraints. Beat a chapter break differently if the prose demands it.
