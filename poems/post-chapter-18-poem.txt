The Note at the Bottom

A seal at the bottom of a file
is a small thing.
Ink, a date, authorization
that takes less time to apply
than the decision took to reach.

She knows this.
She has filed things herself.

What she came in carrying
was the shape of a wrong —
the archive, the buried trail,
the preliminary methodology
with the seal at the bottom
of the note that ended it.

What she found instead
was a man who had been looking
at the same place
for twelve years
and found the same answer each time
and was not entirely at peace
with the fact that the answer
might still be wrong.

*I made it carefully,* he said.
*That is different from making it wrongly.*

She did not find the seam.
She found a man who knew where it was
and had not looked away from it.

Outside, the drainage channel.
The city in its ordinary rain.

She left with the drive.
She crossed the bridge.

In the lobby, a terminal:
one word on the screen,
then nothing.

The gap still there.
The drive still in her pocket.
The rain still finding the joints in the concrete
and running along them
before the drain claimed it.
