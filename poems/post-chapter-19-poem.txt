What He Used

The room: a seam where two constructions met.
The catch pan at its slow work.
The sound of water finding the joint.

He had been losing language for months —
nouns first, then the linkages between them,
grammar retreating the way water retreats
before it finds the lower way.

He had kept some back.
He spent it here.

*I don't want you to become this.*

Five words in their necessary order.
She sat with them.
The room sat with them.
The catch pan received its next drop.

She moved her hand from her lap
and set it open on the floor,
palm up.

His head shifted — a fraction —
toward the warmth of it.
Held.

She said his name, after a while.
Not to call him back.
The way you say a word
just to have it in the air.

Outside: unchanged rain.
The city in its ordinary dark.

She left holding five words
and did not try to put them down.
She moved when she was ready,
and not before.
